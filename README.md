## 01. Introduction

I am Otieno, a student, developer and aspiring inventor with a deep interest in understanding technology and exploring what can be built with it.

My interests extend beyond writing software. I am fascinated by the underlying principles that make technology possible: computation, intelligence, physical systems, algorithms and the interaction between humans and machines.

I enjoy experimenting with ideas, learning unfamiliar concepts and attempting to turn abstract thoughts into practical implementations.

I am particularly interested in:

- **Computer Science:** Programming, algorithms, computational thinking, software architecture and programming language design.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Machine learning, intelligent agents, adaptive systems and artificial life.
- **Physics:** Understanding the fundamental principles governing the physical world.
- **Engineering:** Designing systems, solving technical problems and translating concepts into working prototypes.
- **Innovation:** Exploring unconventional approaches and creating things that challenge existing limitations.

I am still learning and developing my technical foundation. I believe in understanding the fundamentals, experimenting consistently and improving through implementation.

My long-term ambition is to become an inventor who can work across different fields of technology rather than limit creativity to one discipline.

---

## 02. Development Philosophy

I believe that meaningful innovation begins with curiosity, but curiosity alone is not enough. Ideas need to be tested, challenged and transformed into something tangible.

My approach to development is built around five principles:

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Curiosity | Ask questions and investigate how things work. |
| Fundamentals | Understand the underlying concepts, not just the tools. |
| Experimentation | Turn ideas into small, testable prototypes. |
| Iteration | Learn from mistakes and continuously improve. |
| Innovation | Explore possibilities beyond conventional solutions. |

> Always start small. Think beyond limits. Build what doesn't exist.

### EVA — Evolutionary Virtual Android

[Repository: EVA-OFO-001-THE-CORE-](https://github.com/Otieno2/EVA-OFO-001-THE-CORE-)

**Domain:** Artificial Intelligence / Experimental Systems

EVA stands for Evolutionary Virtual Android. OFO-001 represents Origin of Future Organisms, Prototype 001.

The project explores the foundations of an evolving digital entity capable of learning, adapting and developing through interaction with its environment.

The broader research direction involves:

- Adaptive and learning systems.
- Memory and knowledge representation.
- Autonomous behaviour and decision-making.
- Artificial life and machine cognition.
- Hybrid intelligent architectures.

The repository contains Python, Rust and C++ project configuration alongside its core project structure.

**Primary interest:** Exploring how intelligent digital systems could develop beyond conventional reactive chatbot architectures.
